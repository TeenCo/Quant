# Reading Access and Local Assets

This inventory distinguishes material that can be read online from files that are clearly licensed for local redistribution. A public URL or downloadable PDF does **not** by itself establish permission to copy it into the vault. Link to sources by default; download only where the license/terms explicitly allow the intended use.

## Open online material

- [[Sources/Forecasting Principles and Practice]] — Hyndman & Athanasopoulos, *Forecasting: Principles and Practice*, third edition; free online textbook at [OTexts](https://otexts.com/fpp3/). The source note links the online text; no local copy is required.
- [[Sources/Athey Imbens 2017]] — academic review/preprint with an [arXiv abstract and PDF](https://arxiv.org/abs/1607.00699). Verify the current arXiv license before saving a local copy or figures.
- [[Sources/Bacry Mastromatteo Muzy 2015]] — academic review/preprint with [arXiv abstract and PDF](https://arxiv.org/abs/1502.04592). Verify license for local redistribution.
- [[Sources/Rolnick et al 2019]] — climate/ML review with an [arXiv page](https://arxiv.org/abs/1906.05433); link and summarize, and confirm license before local copying.
- [[Sources/Harvey Liu Zhu 2016]], [[Sources/Novy-Marx 2015]], [[Sources/Diebold Mariano 1995]], [[Sources/Moreira Muir 2017]], [[Sources/Obizhaeva Wang 2005]], and other academic sources link to publisher/repository/NBER records. Availability of a working-paper PDF does not automatically authorize local distribution.

## User-provided or permission-dependent

- Marcos López de Prado, *Advances in Financial Machine Learning* — copyrighted book; see [[Sources/Financial ML field notes]]. Obtain via a lawful library/publisher copy; provide a copy only if licensed for this use.
- Cboe methodology/product PDFs and historical data — see [[Sources/Cboe VIX methodology and data]]. The source pages link official materials, but current terms govern retention and reuse; no local copies included.
- Cboe/NBER/publisher working papers, exchange feed documentation, commercial datasets, and vendor reports — link and summarize unless an explicit license permits the specific local use and redistribution.
- London Strategic Edge data — SDK and data terms are distinct; provider terms restrict redistribution. The SDK itself is MIT-licensed, but that does not license downloaded market data. See [[Data/London Strategic Edge]] and [[Sources/LSE data SDK]].

## Figures and diagrams

No third-party figures were copied into the vault in this pass. For educational diagrams, prefer creating original schematics (e.g., point-in-time data lineage, contract roll timeline, or option payoff) and label them as original illustrations. If reproducing a published figure, record author, source, exact license/permission, and modifications in a nearby caption.

## Maintenance rule

Before adding any PDF/image locally, record its source URL, author, publication/version, license/permission, retrieval date, and intended audience/use. If these are unclear, retain a link and summary rather than copying the file.
