## Why it is useful

The text provides an accessible forecasting workflow covering time-series visualization, decomposition/features, regression, exponential smoothing, ARIMA, dynamic regression, hierarchical forecasting, and practical forecasting issues. It emphasizes understanding the series before selecting a model and uses graphical diagnostics.

## Finance adaptations

- Replace shuffled train/test splits with chronological rolling-origin evaluation.
- Include naive baselines and explicit forecast horizons.
- Account for overlapping outcomes, nonstationarity, revisions, and market-specific costs.
- Forecast accuracy does not equal net trading value; map forecasts to decisions separately.
