## Summary and data-research relevance

The paper maps potential ML applications in climate mitigation and adaptation, emphasizing collaboration with domain experts. For quant research it offers non-financial data hypotheses: energy demand, weather, transport, land-use, disaster, and industrial activity may connect to commodity, utility, insurance, infrastructure, or regional macro exposures.

## Limitations and research use

The paper is not evidence that any climate proxy predicts market returns. Translate each domain problem into a well-defined data-generating process, release/arrival timing, affected instruments, mechanism, and falsifiable market prediction. Account for climate data revisions, spatial coverage, and long lag structures.
